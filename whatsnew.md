## 5.9.2

* **The Oryn Wiki is now inside the app.** Open About App and tap Wiki to browse every page without leaving Oryn. It follows your app theme.
* **Find things fast.** Pages are grouped in a sidebar, and every page can be expanded to show the sections inside it. Filter by name, or press Enter to search the text of all pages.
* **Move through pages easily.** Previous and Next buttons sit at the bottom of every page.
* **Download any page as a PDF.** Tap PDF at the top to save the page you are reading. Long pages take a little while to prepare.
* The Wiki needs an internet connection.
